About Me

Warwick Mathematics & Statistics student with a passion for
multivariate analysis and global data solutions.

I am a third-year Integrated Masters (MMathStat) student at the University of Warwick. Originally from Sheffield, I have spent my academic career diving deep into the mathematical theory that powers modern data science. I don’t just apply algorithms; I aim to understand the underlying distributions and statistical mechanics that make them work.

My interests in data science are diverse, ranging from agricultural and environmental modeling to biological research. I am particularly motivated by the potential for data to solve real-world problems. However, my most enduring passion is Sports Analytics. I enjoy taking advanced techniques—like the PCA and K-Means clustering used in my Premier League Audit—and applying them to the beautiful game to uncover structural truths that the league table might hide.

Technical Toolkit & Interests

I love the process of learning new methodologies. My current toolkit includes:

  • Advanced Statistics: GLMs, Multivariate Analysis, Hypothesis Testing, and Machine Learning.
  • Programming: R, Python (Pandas/Scraping), and SQL.
  • Strategic Interests: I am particularly interested in how statistical modeling can improve sustainability in agriculture and efficiency in sports scouting.

Beyond the Numbers

When I’m not analysing datasets, I’m likely involved in football in a more literal sense. I run the Statistics Society Fantasy Football League at Warwick, and I’m a regular on the pitch myself.

My loyalties lie with Forest Green Rovers—a choice made back in Year 8 when a friend and I decided to back a rising League Two underdog rather than the local Sheffield teams. This early interest in "lower league" dynamics fueled my fascination with how clubs grow and the "scale gaps" they face.

Outside of football, I am an avid cook, a runner, and a gym-goer. I also love exploring the world; traveling has given me a broader perspective on how data and technology vary across different cultures and environments.
